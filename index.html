Section 02 // The Mission

About HackHeist

about_hackheist.sh

HackHeist is the premier developer-centric symposium and hackathon organized by GDG on Campus MIET. Designed as an adrenaline-fueled arena of discovery, HackHeist invites student developers, designers, and innovators to learn modern technologies, build impactful solutions, collaborate with peer creators, and connect directly with the tech industry through hands-on workshops, keynotes, and a 24-hour sprint.

Whether you are crafting your first web app or training complex machine learning models, HackHeist gives you the platform, mentors, and resources to push past conventional boundaries and pull off your greatest technical feat.

✓ Collaborative team sprints on real-world problem statements
✓ Practical learning tracks in Web, AI/ML, Cloud, and Open Source
✓ Direct interaction with Google Developer Experts and alumni mentors
24+ Hours of Hacking Non-stop building & ideation
500+ Passionate Hackers Students across institutions
₹50K+ Prizes & Perks Cash, bounties & cloud credits
10+ Mentors & Judges Experienced industry veterans
